# Day 7 – AI Data Lifecycle, Versioning & Retraining

## 1. AI Data Lifecycle

The AI data lifecycle describes how Zecpath data moves from initial upload through AI processing and finally to the hiring decision.

```text
Resume / Job Description Upload
              ↓
        Raw Data Storage
              ↓
           Parsing
              ↓
      Structured AI Data
              ↓
       AI Matching / ATS
              ↓
          ATS Score
              ↓
        AI Screening
              ↓
      Screening Report
              ↓
        AI Interview
              ↓
      Interview Results
              ↓
       Hiring Decision
              ↓
       Retention / Archive
