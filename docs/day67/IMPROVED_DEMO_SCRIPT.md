# Day 67 - Improved Demo Presentation Script

## 1. Opening - 1 Minute

Good morning everyone.

Today I am presenting Zecpath AI, an AI-powered job portal and automated hiring platform.

The project focuses on organizing candidate evaluation and supporting recruitment through specialized AI modules.

## 2. Problem Statement - 1 Minute

Recruitment involves several activities, including reviewing resumes, identifying skills, comparing candidates and conducting interviews.

Handling these activities manually can require significant time and effort.

Zecpath AI aims to organize these activities through a structured workflow.

## 3. AI Solution - 2 Minutes

The platform includes specialized components for:

- Resume parsing.
- Skill extraction.
- Experience parsing.
- Education parsing.
- Semantic matching.
- ATS scoring.
- Candidate ranking.
- Screening and interview support.

Each component handles a specific responsibility.

## 4. System Architecture - 2 Minutes

The frontend communicates with backend services.

The backend coordinates AI processing and data storage.

The modular architecture separates different recruitment responsibilities.

## 5. ATS Scoring - 2 Minutes

The documented ATS scoring components include skill match, experience relevance, education alignment and semantic similarity.

The scoring engine produces an overall candidate score.

The ranking engine organizes candidates using the generated scores.

## 6. Practical Demonstration - 4 Minutes

Show the Day 63 synthetic dataset.

Demonstration sequence:

1. Open a sample candidate resume.
2. Explain the candidate information.
3. Open the corresponding candidate profile.
4. Show the job description.
5. Explain ATS scoring components.
6. Display simulated ATS results.
7. Show screening responses.
8. Show interview responses.
9. Explain the hiring pipeline.

Clearly identify simulated results.

## 7. Business Impact - 1 Minute

The platform aims to reduce repetitive review activities, organize candidate information and provide structured evaluation support.

These are expected benefits, not measured results.

## 8. Future Enhancements - 1 Minute

Potential future developments include interview coaching, improved analytics, real-time feedback and performance optimization.

## 9. Closing - 1 Minute

Zecpath AI demonstrates how specialized AI components can support different stages of recruitment.

The project provides a structured foundation for AI-assisted candidate analysis and recruitment workflow management.

Thank you.

## Presentation Rules

- Speak clearly and at a steady pace.
- Do not read every slide word for word.
- Explain diagrams using the actual workflow.
- Use practical examples.
- Do not exaggerate implementation status.
- Keep answers short and relevant.
