# Day 55 - Governance Documentation

## Data governance

Candidate data must be used only for its recorded purpose and under an applicable consent or other valid legal basis.

## Audit governance

Score and decision events are recorded with actor, timestamp, resource and event details.

## Access governance

Users should receive only the permissions needed for their responsibilities.

## Retention governance

Transcript and report retention periods are configured and enforced by the retention process. Audit log retention requires a separately approved archival and deletion procedure.

## Human oversight

AI scores and recommendations support authorized reviewers. They must not independently make final employment decisions.

## Review and approval

Retention durations, consent wording, access roles and processing purposes must be reviewed by the organization's privacy, security and HR owners before production use.
