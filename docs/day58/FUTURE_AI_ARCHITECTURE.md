# Future AI Architecture

## 1. Overview
Proposed architecture improvements for the Zecpath AI hiring platform.

## 2. AI Video Analysis
- Analyze candidate video interviews.
- Evaluate communication and response delivery.
- Generate structured interview insights.

## 3. Emotion Detection
- Explore responsible emotion-related signal analysis.
- Avoid treating inferred emotions as definitive evidence.
- Include human review.

## 4. Real-Time Feedback
- Provide interview progress indicators.
- Generate relevant feedback after responses.
- Support interviewer decision-making.

## 5. AI Coaching System
- Identify candidate improvement areas.
- Recommend interview practice topics.
- Provide personalized improvement suggestions.

## 6. Interview Analytics Dashboard
- Interview performance summaries.
- Skill and competency insights.
- Candidate comparison support.

## 7. Scaling Roadmap
- Phase 1: Improve existing AI services.
- Phase 2: Introduce video analysis and coaching.
- Phase 3: Add analytics and real-time feedback.
- Phase 4: Optimize infrastructure for increased usage.

## 8. Conclusion
These are proposed future enhancements, subject to feasibility, testing, privacy, and responsible AI review.
