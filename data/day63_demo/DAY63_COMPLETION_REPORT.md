# Day 63 - Demo Dataset Creation

## Objective

Prepare realistic fictional data to demonstrate the Zecpath AI hiring workflow.

## Dataset Created

- Candidate resumes with varied quality.
- Job descriptions for three different roles.
- Structured candidate profiles.
- Candidate screening responses.
- Simulated ATS scores and decisions.
- Simulated screening outputs.
- HR and technical interview responses.
- End-to-end hiring pipeline records.

## Dataset Characteristics

- 10 fictional candidates.
- 3 job descriptions.
- 10 resumes.
- 10 screening responses.
- 10 ATS result records.
- 10 screening output records.
- 20 interview response records.
- 10 hiring pipeline records.

## Important Note

ATS scores and screening outcomes are simulated demonstration data.
They are not generated by the production AI models.

## Outcome

A structured synthetic dataset has been prepared for demonstrating
candidate processing and the end-to-end recruitment workflow.
